## Prevention changes

- `_unnumbered_repeat`: one account, one exact start, one named venue. A
  meeting or schedule-day label only one title carries (“GM#1”, “Recruitment
  Day 3”) and an end only one post prints no longer separate the listings.
  Different numbers, “Session 2”-style divisions, rooms, ends and owners still do.
- “Student Recreation Center”, “Student Rec Center” and “Rec Center” spell SRC.
- `_shared_occasion_name`: different accounts, identical start, compatible
  ends, the same named venue and three shared distinctive title words (Latin,
  Latinx and Latino normalized). Titles phrased “X at Y”, a club's part in a
  larger occasion, are excluded.
- A deadline's season qualifier no longer separates one account's timed and
  date-only cutoff that day; two different seasons still do.
- Category inference ignores member-profile slides (two or more of HOBBIES,
  FUN FACT, FAVORITE …, Major, Pronouns, …). Food, signup and kind policy
  still read every slide.

Across 19,800 candidate pairs from four audit snapshots (1,426 rows), the new
rules add 12 matches and remove none. All 12 are repeats; the six past ones
(HBS, HISA, MEChA, CFlow, Student Org Awards) are outside the planner's
upcoming window and were not changed. The profile filter changes 3 of 1,650
replayed occurrence categories, all corrections (the two CSA mixers and a
Bible study filed under sports).
