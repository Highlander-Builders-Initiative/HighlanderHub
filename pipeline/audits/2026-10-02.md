## Prevention changes

- Recognize plural event/meeting schedule headings and contiguous labeled
  numeric date tiles. Fractions, room numbers, and unlabeled numeric blocks
  remain unsupported.
- Honor explicit location updates within matching multi-session schedules.
  Repeated publication carries the corrected venue along with the source.
  Different owners, starts, numbered sessions and admin “different” decisions
  remain protected.
- Store attributed visual OCR corrections inside reviewed assessments while
  preserving original text and fingerprints. Remote cached replay uses the
  review; a changed source or explicit refresh expires it.
- Keep a sibling bake sale from suppressing independently cited activities.
  Recognize “pizza included” as a provision while retaining price and negation
  checks.
- Preserve a merged venue during unchanged publication when an accepted
  current repost still supplies that exact venue. Full-archive replay exposed
  Fall Reception losing its museum venue when multiple reposts used different
  spellings or an address. Unsupported saved venues and changed owner decisions
  do not receive this fallback.

## Addendum: Ride to The Cheech

UCR TAPS advertised one October 4 trip twice: a flyer post (check in 11:50 a.m.)
and the October carousel (leave UCR at 12 p.m.). The model split each post into
the trip plus an “IE Zine Fest 26'” occurrence, because the caption and a slide
describe what The Cheech hosts that afternoon. The flyer's Zine Fest row carried
the Ride to The Cheech flyer image. The two trip rows never merged: same-day
listings with different timed starts were always treated as separate sessions,
though both shared the venue and the `events.ucr.edu/event/ride-to-the-cheech`
signup link.

- Recorded source reviews for `3997715456455231193` and `3997897538102448491`
  that drop the destination's Zine Fest occurrence. Deleted the locked Zine Fest
  listing with a deletion marker; MEChA's own IE Zine Fest Social is unaffected.
- Merged the carousel's 12 p.m. trip into the locked 11:50 a.m. listing through
  the merge RPC, so the decision holds under the deployed code.
- Prevention: the assessment prompt treats a trip's destination program as part
  of the trip (`VERSION = 10`), and `same_event` merges one account's listings at
  the same venue and signup link that start at most 15 minutes apart. Across the
  archive, that rule matches only this pair.
- The two flyer rows were locked by the September 30 metadata repair, not by an
  admin edit; that repair locked all ten rows it corrected.
