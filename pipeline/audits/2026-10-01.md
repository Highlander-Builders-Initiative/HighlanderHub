# October 1 pipeline and publication audit

Audited the recent hosted runs, saved captions and carousel OCR, assessment
responses (including rejected responses), source ownership, current event rows,
admin decisions, and notification history. The latest two collection runs
succeeded, but successful stages had still published incorrect or incomplete
event data. Prevention fixes are local; live source/data repairs are verified.

## Evidence and coverage

The initial snapshot at 2026-10-02 06:05 UTC contained 1,223 events, 3,765 source
assessments (2,952 posts and 813 legacy stories), 2,958 archived posts and their
extraction records, 28 deletion markers, and 33 locked events. Reviewed the 413
assessments made since September 30 and checked source support and duplicate
candidates across 554 current/future events. Visually checked the saved Tau Beta
Pi, HEARTS, and hyperpop flyers where OCR or layout mattered.

Current collection is posts only. Legacy story assessments still contribute
source support, but these runs do not collect new stories or profile bios.
References to “link in bio” in captions are not snapshots of the bio itself.

Private backups, raw logs, repair plans, and before/after verification are in
the gitignored `pipeline/data/repairs/20261001-audit/` directory. Raw database
snapshots are not part of this change.

## Prevention changes

- Scope classification to the individual caption session while retaining the
  full displayed caption. Flyer fine print cannot classify sibling sessions
  as fundraisers. A caption only one session cites keeps everything except
  the sibling fundraiser's paragraphs, so its date, venue and food offers
  still count. Blood donation does not count as a monetary donation, but
  unrelated fundraising requests still do.
- Retain shared RSVP instructions and detect explicit snacks provisions using
  the existing negation and price checks.
- Require explicitly dated application cutoffs to publish as deadlines. The
  assessment instructions distinguish independent action cutoffs, agenda
  items, continuous fairs, and separate registered sessions. The cutoff's
  date must be on its own line, or on the next line under a bare heading;
  a past-tense “applications closed” is not an upcoming cutoff.
- Support explicit next-week activity weekdays and the visually verified
  October OCR spelling. Only the weekdays in the “next week on …” clause
  are dated; a standing “every Monday” elsewhere is not. Bare weekdays and
  missing AM/PM remain unsupported.
- Detect clock conflicts in separately headed paragraphs of monthly captions.
  Explicit caption time corrections must be cited and their corrected clocks
  must match; the older flyer cannot silently override them.
- Match accents consistently, numbered schedule entries with their unnumbered
  reminders, explicit venue revisions, and corroborated conference teasers.
  Different owners, explicit session numbers, unlabelled venue disagreements,
  and separate fair/tour offerings remain distinct. Deadline program years
  are compared separately from the cutoff year.

Assessment version 9 records the policy change. It does not invalidate the
entire archive or initiate a paid reassessment of old cached sources.

## Remaining boundaries

Thirteen candidate pairs remain for review. Most are different clubs,
different speakers or independent actions. The R’Garden festival posts have
conflicting clocks without an explicit correction; CNAS prints “Spring” on a
waiver deadline while the Business guide explicitly says Fall. Those are
source disagreements, not grounds for a silent automatic merge.

There are 617 `expired_media` extraction records and five `no_text` records in
the initial archive. Expired media are intentionally terminal under the
current no-refresh policy; they limit extraction coverage and are not proof
that the source contained no event. No paid archive refresh was performed.
New stories and actual profile bio contents are outside current collection.

The code changes and tests are local and uncommitted. They have not been
deployed to Actions. The saved source reviews and merge decisions are durable,
but code-dependent fixes such as blood-drive and sibling-fundraiser mapping
need deployment to prevent the old hosted mapper from undoing them.
