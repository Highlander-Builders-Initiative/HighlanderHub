## Prevention changes

- Ground application-cutoff numeric dates and Korean month/day dates, including
  explicit years. Preserve fraction, room-number, and unsupported-year guards.
- Preserve a weekday-labeled date when the following OCR line starts with
  “of”, as in “Psychology of Gratitude”.
- Clarify shared AM/PM session windows, date-only club activities, calendar
  themes, and venue-information pointers in the assessment prompt. Reject the
  observed “location: check Canvas” venue inference in validation.
- Recognize explicitly labeled meal offers and avoid treating admission-priced
  included drinks/food as free merely because OCR places them on another line.
- Match a numbered tryout reminder and an explicitly revised guest-speaker
  schedule without collapsing different owners, times, or session numbers.
- Honor a saved merge when an incomplete schedule row also matches competing
  details. Replay previously recreated Chemistry's reviewed duplicate because
  ambiguity filtering ran before the explicit review. Only the selected
  survivor bypasses that filter; changed sources and missing survivors still
  publish for fresh reconciliation, and sibling sessions remain independent.
- Re-encode oversized storage flyers as bounded JPEGs. The failed soccer
  image (`3999243499157264562`, slide 20) was recovered at 1,640,004 bytes,
  down from 5,255,986 bytes. OCR was not repeated; smaller images are unchanged.

Prompt changes guide future assessments; they do not demonstrate that a model
will always interpret a visual calendar correctly. The concrete repairs use
reviewed decisions and saved visual evidence.
